Dear Diary

This is a blank page. Write whatever is on your mind. Nothing you write leaves this Mac unless you ask it to.

It is built on freewrite by Farza, and everything freewrite does is still here: the timer, the fonts, video entries, backspace off, History.

Three things are new, and all of them are off until you want them.

Talk. Write first, then click Talk to think it through with an AI that has read the page. It asks more than it tells.

Memory. The AI can read each entry once you have finished with it and keep notes on what keeps coming up. Click Memory to see them, each one traced back to your own words.

Versions. A copy of an entry every five minutes while it changes, so you can go back.

To turn any of these on, open Settings from the Dear Diary menu, or press ⌘ and comma. The AI can run on this Mac for free, or through OpenAI or Claude with your own key.

If you used freewrite before, Settings can also bring your old entries across.

When you are ready, click New Entry and start.
