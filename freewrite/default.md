Dear Diary

This is a blank page. Write whatever is on your mind. Nothing you write leaves this Mac unless you ask it to.

The bar below has a timer, fonts and a camera for video entries. History opens from its left end.

Three more things are here when you want them. All of them are off until you turn them on.

Talk. Write first, then click the speech bubble below to think it through with an AI that has read the page. It asks more than it tells.

Memory. The AI can read each entry once you have finished with it and keep notes on what keeps coming up. Click the joined dots below to see them, each one traced back to your own words.

Versions. A copy of an entry every five minutes while it changes, so you can go back.

To turn any of these on, open Settings from the Dear Diary menu, or press ⌘ and comma. The AI can run on this Mac for free, or through OpenAI or Claude with your own key.

When you are ready, click the pencil below for a new entry and start.

Built on freewrite by Farza. If you used freewrite before, Settings can bring your old entries across.
